# Australia — Australian Defence Force (ADF)

![Australia Flag](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/88/Flag_of_Australia_%28converted%29.svg/320px-Flag_of_Australia_%28converted%29.svg.png)

**Official Name:** Australian Defence Force  
**Active Personnel (approx.):** ~60,000

Highly professional, all-volunteer force with strong expeditionary and alliance focus (ANZUS, AUKUS, Five Eyes).

**Main HQ:** Russell Offices / Department of Defence — Canberra.

**Branches:** Australian Army, Royal Australian Navy, Royal Australian Air Force. Growing emphasis on long-range strike, undersea warfare, and regional presence.
