# Canada — Canadian Armed Forces

![Canada Flag](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/d9/Flag_of_Canada_%28Pantone%29.svg/320px-Flag_of_Canada_%28Pantone%29.svg.png)

**Official Name:** Canadian Armed Forces / Forces armées canadiennes  
**Active Personnel (approx.):** ~70,000

Unified structure (Army, Navy, Air Force under single command), strong NATO and NORAD commitments, Arctic focus, and expeditionary experience.

**Main HQ:** National Defence Headquarters — Ottawa.

**Branches:** Canadian Army, Royal Canadian Navy, Royal Canadian Air Force (plus unified elements).
