# People's Liberation Army (PLA) — China

![China Flag](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/fa/Flag_of_the_People%27s_Republic_of_China.svg/320px-Flag_of_the_People%27s_Republic_of_China.svg.png)

**Official Name:** People's Liberation Army (PLA)  
**Supreme Command:** Central Military Commission (CMC) of the Communist Party of China  
**Chairman of CMC:** President / General Secretary  
**Active Personnel (approx.):** ~2 million (largest standing military by active personnel)

The PLA is the armed wing of the Chinese Communist Party and the primary military force of the People's Republic of China. It has undergone major modernization and reorganization since the 2010s.

---

## Main Headquarters

- **Central Military Commission (CMC)** — Beijing  
  Highest military leadership body. Exercises absolute leadership over the PLA.

- **Ministry of National Defense** — Beijing (largely administrative/diplomatic role).

- **Theater Commands** (reorganized 2016):  
  Eastern, Southern, Western, Northern, Central Theater Commands. These are joint operational commands.

### Key Bases & Facilities
- Major naval bases: Qingdao (North Sea Fleet), Ningbo/Zhoushan (East Sea Fleet), Zhanjiang (South Sea Fleet).
- Air and rocket force bases distributed across the country.
- Historic/symbolic: Various revolutionary-era sites; modern underground facilities and island bases in the South China Sea.

## Organization (Post-2015/2016 Reforms)

**Services:**
1. **PLA Ground Force**
2. **PLA Navy** (including Marines and Naval Aviation)
3. **PLA Air Force**
4. **PLA Rocket Force** (strategic and conventional missiles)
5. **PLA Strategic Support Force** (now largely reorganized into Information Support, Cyber, and Aerospace forces)
6. **Joint Logistics Support Force**

Theater Commands control joint operations in their geographic areas. The CMC retains direct control over certain strategic assets (Rocket Force, strategic support elements).

---

## Notable Details

- Rapid modernization: aircraft carriers, stealth fighters, hypersonic missiles, large navy expansion.
- Emphasis on "informatized" and "intelligentized" warfare.
- Conscription exists but the force is largely professional/volunteer in practice for key units.
- Political commissar system runs parallel to the command chain.
