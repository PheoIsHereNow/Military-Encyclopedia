# French Armed Forces (Armées françaises)

![France Flag](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/thumb/c/c3/Flag_of_France.svg/320px-Flag_of_France.svg.png)

**Official Name:** Armées françaises  
**Commander-in-Chief:** President of the Republic  
**Ministry:** Ministère des Armées  
**Active Personnel (approx.):** ~200,000–220,000

France maintains a full-spectrum force including an independent nuclear deterrent (Force de dissuasion), expeditionary capabilities, and significant overseas presence.

---

## Main Headquarters

- **Ministère des Armées** — Paris (Hôtel de Brienne / Balard complex)
- **État-Major des Armées (EMA)** — Paris  
  Joint Chiefs equivalent.
- Major bases: Toulon (Mediterranean Fleet), Brest (Atlantic), Istres, etc.
- Historic: Les Invalides (ceremonial and museum), various Vauban fortifications.

---

## Rank Structure (Army)

### Officers
- Général d'armée (OF-9)
- Général de corps d'armée (OF-8)
- Général de division (OF-7)
- Général de brigade (OF-6)
- Colonel, Lieutenant-colonel, Commandant, Capitaine, Lieutenant, Sous-lieutenant

### Other Ranks
- Major (highest NCO in some contexts), Adjudant-chef, Adjudant, Sergent-chef, Sergent, Caporal-chef, Caporal, Soldat

France uses a distinctive star system for generals (more stars than many peers for equivalent ranks).

---

## Branches

1. **Armée de Terre** (Army)
2. **Marine Nationale** (Navy)
3. **Armée de l'Air et de l'Espace** (Air & Space Force)
4. **Gendarmerie Nationale** (military police with dual role)

Nuclear forces under the Strategic Oceanic Force (FOST) and Strategic Air Forces.
