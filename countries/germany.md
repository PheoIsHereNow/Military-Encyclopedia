# Germany — Bundeswehr

![Germany Flag](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/thumb/b/ba/Flag_of_Germany.svg/320px-Flag_of_Germany.svg.png)

**Official Name:** Bundeswehr  
**Supreme Command:** Federal Minister of Defence (political); Inspector General of the Bundeswehr  
**Active Personnel (approx.):** ~180,000–200,000

Germany maintains a professional, NATO-integrated force focused on collective defence, with increasing emphasis on readiness and modernization following Russia’s full-scale invasion of Ukraine.

---

## Main Headquarters

- **Federal Ministry of Defence** — Bendlerblock / Hardthöhe, Berlin / Bonn area.
- **Bundeswehr Joint Forces Operations Command** and service headquarters.
- Major bases: multiple across Germany; significant presence in NATO structures.

---

## Rank Structure (Army focus)

Officers follow a standard NATO-aligned system: General, Generalleutnant, Generalmajor, Brigadegeneral, Oberst, Oberstleutnant, Major, Hauptmann, Oberleutnant, Leutnant.

Enlisted/NCO: Oberstabsfeldwebel down to Schütze (Private).

---

## Branches

- Heer (Army)
- Marine (Navy)
- Luftwaffe (Air Force)
- Streitkräftebasis (Joint Support)
- Cyber- und Informationsraum

Strong parliamentary control and historical restrictions on expeditionary use have eased in recent years.
