# Indian Armed Forces

![India Flag](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/thumb/4/41/Flag_of_India.svg/320px-Flag_of_India.svg.png)

**Official Name:** Indian Armed Forces  
**Supreme Commander:** President of India  
**Ministry:** Ministry of Defence  
**Active Personnel (approx.):** ~1.4 million (one of the largest)

India maintains a large, professional all-volunteer force focused on continental defence, with growing naval and air power projection, and a nuclear triad under development/completion.

---

## Main Headquarters

- **Integrated Defence Staff Headquarters** and service HQs in New Delhi / South Block area.
- **Army Headquarters** — New Delhi.
- Major commands: Northern, Western, Eastern, Southern, Central, South Western Commands.
- Key bases: Various cantonments (e.g., Ambala, Jalandhar, Pathankot), naval bases at Mumbai, Visakhapatnam, Karwar; air bases across the country.
- Historic forts: Many (Red Fort symbolic, numerous hill forts and British-era cantonments still in use).

## Rank Structure (Army)

Closely follows British-derived system with local titles.

### Officers
- Field Marshal (ceremonial)
- General
- Lieutenant General
- Major General
- Brigadier
- Colonel
- Lieutenant Colonel
- Major
- Captain
- Lieutenant
- Second Lieutenant

### Other Ranks
- Subedar Major / Risaldar Major
- Subedar / Risaldar
- Naib Subedar
- Havildar
- Naik
- Lance Naik
- Sepoy (or equivalent: Sowar, Gunner, etc.)
