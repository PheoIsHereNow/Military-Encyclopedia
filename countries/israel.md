# Israel — Israel Defense Forces (IDF)

![Israel Flag](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/d4/Flag_of_Israel.svg/320px-Flag_of_Israel.svg.png)

**Official Name:** Israel Defense Forces (Tzahal)  
**Supreme Command:** Prime Minister / Minister of Defense; Chief of the General Staff  
**Active Personnel (approx.):** ~170,000 (with large, well-trained reserves)

A highly capable, conscript-based force with exceptional operational experience, advanced technology (especially intelligence, air power, missile defense, and cyber), and a doctrine emphasizing rapid mobilization and qualitative edge.

---

## Main Headquarters

- **Kirya** (HaKirya) — Tel Aviv — main military headquarters complex.
- Regional commands and major bases throughout the country.

---

## Rank Structure

Officers: Rav Aluf (Chief of Staff / Lt Gen equivalent), Aluf, Tat Aluf, Aluf Mishne, Sgan Aluf, etc.

Enlisted ranks are distinct; strong NCO and reserve culture.

---

## Branches

- Ground Forces
- Air Force (very high readiness and quality)
- Navy
- Intelligence Directorate (Aman) and other specialized units play outsized roles.

Mandatory service for most citizens; large and frequently mobilized reserve system.
