# Japan — Japan Self-Defense Forces (JSDF)

![Japan Flag](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/thumb/9/9e/Flag_of_Japan.svg/320px-Flag_of_Japan.svg.png)

**Official Name:** Japan Self-Defense Forces (Jieitai)  
**Supreme Command:** Prime Minister (Commander-in-Chief); Minister of Defense  
**Active Personnel (approx.):** ~240,000–250,000

Constrained by Article 9 of the Constitution, the JSDF is oriented toward exclusive defense, though interpretation and capabilities have expanded significantly in recent decades (especially maritime and air power, missile defense, and counter-strike discussions).

---

## Main Headquarters

- **Ministry of Defense** — Tokyo.
- **Joint Staff** and service staffs.
- Key bases: Yokosuka (naval), various air bases, ground force regional armies.

---

## Rank Structure

Ranks closely parallel U.S./NATO styles with Japanese titles (e.g., Rikushō = General, etc.). Strong emphasis on professionalism and technology.

---

## Branches

- Japan Ground Self-Defense Force (JGSDF)
- Japan Maritime Self-Defense Force (JMSDF) — highly capable navy
- Japan Air Self-Defense Force (JASDF)

Close alliance with the United States; growing security cooperation with Australia, UK, and others.
