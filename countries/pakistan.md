# Pakistan — Pakistan Armed Forces

![Pakistan Flag](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/32/Flag_of_Pakistan.svg/320px-Flag_of_Pakistan.svg.png)

**Official Name:** Pakistan Armed Forces  
**Active Personnel (approx.):** ~650,000+

Professional force with nuclear capability, significant experience in conventional and counter-insurgency operations, and a powerful army tradition.

**Main HQ:** General Headquarters (GHQ) — Rawalpindi.

**Branches:** Pakistan Army, Pakistan Navy, Pakistan Air Force. Strong corps structure and strategic forces.
