# Armed Forces of the Russian Federation

![Russia Flag](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/thumb/f/f3/Flag_of_Russia.svg/320px-Flag_of_Russia.svg.png)

**Official Name:** Armed Forces of the Russian Federation  
**Supreme Commander:** President of the Russian Federation  
**Ministry:** Ministry of Defence  
**Active Personnel (approx.):** ~1.1–1.5 million (estimates vary)

Russia maintains a large, nuclear-armed force with significant conventional capabilities, particularly in artillery, armor, air defense, and strategic rocket forces. Organized under a General Staff system.

---

## Main Headquarters

- **Ministry of Defence of the Russian Federation** — Moscow  
  Main administrative and political headquarters.

- **General Staff of the Armed Forces** — Moscow  
  Operational planning and control.

- **National Defence Management Centre** — Moscow  
  Modern command-and-control hub.

### Key Military Districts & Bases
- Western, Southern, Central, Eastern Military Districts (plus Northern Fleet as joint strategic command).
- Major bases include those around Moscow, St. Petersburg, Sevastopol (Black Sea Fleet), Vladivostok (Pacific Fleet), and strategic sites for nuclear forces.
- Historic fortifications: Kremlin (symbolic/political), various Soviet-era fortresses and modern fortified areas.

## Organization

- **Ground Forces**
- **Navy** (including Naval Infantry)
- **Aerospace Forces** (Air Force + Air and Missile Defence + Space)
- **Strategic Rocket Forces** (nuclear ICBMs)
- **Airborne Forces (VDV)** — elite independent branch
- **Special Operations Forces**

Military Districts control combined-arms armies, air armies, and supporting units.

---

## Notable Details

- Large nuclear triad.
- Emphasis on massed fires, electronic warfare, and combined-arms operations.
- Conscription still exists alongside contract service.
