# South Korea — Republic of Korea Armed Forces

![South Korea Flag](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/09/Flag_of_South_Korea.svg/320px-Flag_of_South_Korea.svg.png)

**Official Name:** Republic of Korea Armed Forces  
**Active Personnel (approx.):** ~500,000 (large conscript force)

Highly capable, technologically advanced force oriented primarily toward deterrence against North Korea, with strong U.S. alliance.

**Main HQ:** Ministry of National Defense and Joint Chiefs of Staff — Seoul area.

**Branches:** Army (largest), Navy (including Marines), Air Force. Excellent reserve system and mandatory service.
