# Turkey — Turkish Armed Forces

![Turkey Flag](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/b4/Flag_of_Turkey.svg/320px-Flag_of_Turkey.svg.png)

**Official Name:** Türk Silahlı Kuvvetleri  
**Active Personnel (approx.):** ~350,000+

NATO member with large conscript/professional force, significant regional power projection, and extensive operational experience.

**Main HQ:** Ministry of National Defense and General Staff — Ankara.

**Branches:** Land Forces, Naval Forces, Air Force. Strong special forces and growing defense industry.
