# Ukraine — Armed Forces of Ukraine

![Ukraine Flag](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/49/Flag_of_Ukraine.svg/320px-Flag_of_Ukraine.svg.png)

**Official Name:** Zbroini Syly Ukrainy  
**Active Personnel (approx.):** Significantly expanded since 2022 (estimates vary widely; large mobilization).

Currently engaged in high-intensity conventional warfare against Russian forces. Rapid adaptation, Western equipment integration, and innovative use of drones and artillery have been defining features.

**Main HQ:** General Staff — Kyiv.

**Branches:** Ground Forces, Air Force, Navy, Air Assault Forces, Special Operations, Territorial Defense, and others.

*Open source compilation. Situation remains highly dynamic.*
