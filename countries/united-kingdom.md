# United Kingdom Armed Forces

![UK Flag](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/thumb/a/ae/Flag_of_the_United_Kingdom.svg/320px-Flag_of_the_United_Kingdom.svg.png)

**Official Name:** British Armed Forces / His Majesty's Armed Forces  
**Commander-in-Chief:** The Monarch (King Charles III)  
**Political Head:** Secretary of State for Defence  
**Active Personnel (approx.):** ~140,000–150,000

The UK maintains a highly professional, expeditionary force with strong traditions, nuclear deterrent (via Royal Navy), and key roles in NATO and global operations.

---

## Main Headquarters & Forts

### Primary Headquarters
- **Ministry of Defence (MoD)** — Main Building, Whitehall, London  
  Central political and strategic control.

- **Horse Guards** — Whitehall, London  
  Historic headquarters associated with Household Division and ceremonial duties; also houses some Army staff functions.

- **Permanent Joint Headquarters (PJHQ)** — Northwood, Hertfordshire  
  Operational command for joint and overseas operations.

### Major Bases & Historic Forts
- **Royal Military Academy Sandhurst** — Officer training.
- **Aldershot** — Historic "Home of the British Army".
- **Catterick Garrison** — One of the largest British Army garrisons.
- **Portsmouth Naval Base** — Major Royal Navy home port.
- **HMNB Clyde (Faslane)** — Submarine base, home of the nuclear deterrent.
- **RAF Brize Norton** — Main air transport hub.
- Historic fortifications: Tower of London (ceremonial), Dover Castle, various coastal forts from Napoleonic and WWII eras.

## Organization

The British Army is organized into:
- **1st (UK) Division** and **3rd (UK) Division** as the primary deployable formations.
- Strong regimental system with deep traditions (Household Division, Parachute Regiment, Royal Marines as separate elite force under Navy).
- Joint Force structure with Permanent Joint Headquarters for operations.

---

## Key Branches

1. **British Army**
2. **Royal Navy** (including Royal Marines)
3. **Royal Air Force**

Nuclear deterrent is provided by the Continuous At-Sea Deterrent (CASD) using Vanguard/Dreadnought-class submarines armed with Trident missiles.
