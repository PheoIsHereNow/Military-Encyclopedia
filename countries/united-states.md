# United States Armed Forces

![US Flag](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/thumb/a/a4/Flag_of_the_United_States.svg/320px-Flag_of_the_United_States.svg.png)

**Official Name:** United States Armed Forces  
**Commander-in-Chief:** President of the United States  
**Department:** Department of Defense (DoD)  
**Active Personnel (approx.):** ~1.3 million  
**Reserve + National Guard:** ~800,000+

The United States maintains one of the most powerful and technologically advanced military forces in the world, with global power projection capabilities across all domains (land, sea, air, space, cyber).

---

## Main Headquarters & Forts

### Primary Headquarters
- **The Pentagon** (Arlington, Virginia)  
  Headquarters of the Department of Defense, Joint Chiefs of Staff, and service secretaries. The world's largest office building by floor area.

  ![Pentagon](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/0c/The_Pentagon_January_2008.jpg/640px-The_Pentagon_January_2008.jpg)

### Major Army Installations
- **Fort Bragg** (North Carolina) — Largest U.S. Army base by population. Home of XVIII Airborne Corps, 82nd Airborne Division, and U.S. Army Special Operations Command. (Previously named Fort Liberty 2023–2025; restored as Fort Bragg honoring WWII soldier Roland L. Bragg.)
- **Fort Cavazos** (Texas) — Formerly Fort Hood; major armored and mechanized force hub.
- **Fort Liberty / Fort Bragg** details: Over 50,000 active-duty personnel; one of the largest military complexes globally.
- **Joint Base Lewis-McChord** (Washington)
- **Fort Campbell** (Kentucky/Tennessee) — Home of 101st Airborne Division.
- **Fort Moore** (Georgia) — Formerly Fort Benning; Infantry School.

### Other Key Sites
- **Norfolk Naval Station** (Virginia) — Largest naval base in the world.
- **Naval Station Pearl Harbor** / Joint Base Pearl Harbor-Hickam (Hawaii)
- **Ramstein Air Base** (Germany) — Major USAFE hub.
- **Camp Humphreys** (South Korea)

## Organization & Hierarchy

Typical Army structure (approximate strengths):

- **Squad** (~9–13) — Sergeant
- **Platoon** (~30–50) — Lieutenant
- **Company** (~100–200) — Captain
- **Battalion** (~300–1,000) — Lieutenant Colonel
- **Brigade** (~3,000–5,000) — Colonel / Brigadier General
- **Division** (~10,000–20,000) — Major General
- **Corps** (~20,000–60,000) — Lieutenant General
- **Field Army** — General

The U.S. military is organized under Unified Combatant Commands (e.g., CENTCOM, INDOPACOM, EUCOM) that are joint and geographic or functional.

---

## Key Branches

1. **U.S. Army** — Land warfare, largest service by personnel.
2. **U.S. Navy** — Sea control, power projection, carrier strike groups.
3. **U.S. Air Force** — Air superiority, strategic bombing, ISR.
4. **U.S. Marine Corps** — Expeditionary force-in-readiness.
5. **U.S. Space Force** — Space domain awareness and operations (established 2019).
6. **U.S. Coast Guard** — Maritime security (under DHS in peacetime).

---

## Notable Details

- Global network of bases and alliances (NATO, AUKUS, bilateral treaties).
- High emphasis on technology, precision munitions, ISR, and joint operations.
- Conscription abolished; all-volunteer force since 1973.
