# Comparative Military Ranks

NATO rank codes provide a standardized way to compare ranks across nations. **OF** = Officer, **OR** = Other Ranks (enlisted/NCO).

**Notes:**
- Exact titles and insignia vary. Some nations (e.g., France, Russia) have unique traditions.
- Warrant officers in the US sit between enlisted and commissioned officers.
- Always refer to national regulations for precise authority and protocol.
