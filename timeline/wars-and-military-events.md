# Timeline of Wars and Important Military Events

A long chronological reference of major wars, campaigns, and pivotal military events from antiquity to the present day. This is not exhaustive — no single list can be — but it aims to be broad, useful, and continuously expandable.

Entries focus on conflicts of significant scale, strategic impact, or historical importance.

---

## Ancient World (c. 3000 BCE – 500 CE)

- **c. 2700–1000 BCE** — Early Mesopotamian, Egyptian, and Indus conflicts; chariot warfare emerges.
- **c. 1274 BCE** — Battle of Kadesh (Egypt vs. Hittites) — one of the earliest well-documented major battles; leads to the first known peace treaty.
- **c. 1200 BCE** — Trojan War (traditional date; historicity debated) and the Late Bronze Age Collapse / Sea Peoples invasions.
- **499–449 BCE** — Greco-Persian Wars (including Marathon 490 BCE, Thermopylae & Salamis 480 BCE, Plataea 479 BCE).
- **431–404 BCE** — Peloponnesian War (Athens vs. Sparta and allies).
- **334–323 BCE** — Wars of Alexander the Great — conquest of the Persian Empire.
- **264–146 BCE** — Punic Wars (Rome vs. Carthage); includes Hannibal’s crossing of the Alps and the destruction of Carthage.
- **58–50 BCE** — Gallic Wars (Julius Caesar).
- **49–45 BCE** — Caesar’s Civil War.
- **27 BCE – 180 CE** — Pax Romana (relative internal peace within the Roman Empire, punctuated by external wars).
- **66–73 CE** — First Jewish–Roman War (includes the siege and destruction of Jerusalem in 70 CE).
- **3rd–5th centuries** — Crisis of the Third Century, Gothic and Hunnic invasions, fall of the Western Roman Empire (476 CE traditionally).

---

## Medieval Period (c. 500–1500)

- **533–554** — Justinian’s wars of reconquest (Vandalic War, Gothic War).
- **7th–8th centuries** — Early Muslim conquests (Levant, Egypt, Persia, North Africa, Iberia).
- **711–1492** — Reconquista in Iberia.
- **793–1100s** — Viking Age raids and wars across Europe.
- **1095–1291** — The Crusades (multiple major expeditions to the Holy Land and beyond).
- **1206–1368** — Mongol conquests under Genghis Khan and successors — one of the largest contiguous land empires in history.
- **1337–1453** — Hundred Years’ War (England vs. France).
- **1453** — Fall of Constantinople to the Ottoman Turks — end of the Byzantine Empire.
- **1455–1485** — Wars of the Roses (England).

---

## Early Modern Period (1500–1800)

- **1494–1559** — Italian Wars.
- **1519–1521 / 1532–1533** — Spanish conquest of the Aztec and Inca empires.
- **1562–1598** — French Wars of Religion.
- **1568–1648** — Eighty Years’ War (Dutch Revolt against Spain).
- **1618–1648** — Thirty Years’ War — devastating conflict in Central Europe.
- **1642–1651** — English Civil Wars.
- **1683** — Battle of Vienna — Ottoman siege lifted by Polish-led relief force.
- **1701–1714** — War of the Spanish Succession.
- **1756–1763** — Seven Years’ War — often considered the first true “world war.”
- **1775–1783** — American Revolutionary War.
- **1789–1799** — French Revolutionary Wars begin.

---

## 19th Century

- **1803–1815** — Napoleonic Wars (including Trafalgar 1805, Austerlitz 1805, Borodino 1812, Waterloo 1815).
- **1812** — War of 1812 (United States vs. Britain).
- **1846–1848** — Mexican–American War.
- **1853–1856** — Crimean War.
- **1861–1865** — American Civil War.
- **1870–1871** — Franco-Prussian War — leads to German unification.
- **1894–1895** — First Sino-Japanese War.
- **1898** — Spanish–American War.
- **1899–1902** — Second Boer War.

---

## Early 20th Century & World Wars

- **1904–1905** — Russo-Japanese War.
- **1912–1913** — Balkan Wars.
- **1914–1918** — **World War I** (the Great War) — trench warfare, mass mobilization, collapse of empires.
- **1917–1922** — Russian Civil War.
- **1931–1945** — Second Sino-Japanese War (merges into WWII).
- **1936–1939** — Spanish Civil War.
- **1939–1945** — **World War II** — the deadliest conflict in human history (Europe, Pacific, North Africa, Asia; includes Holocaust, atomic bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki).

---

## Cold War Era (1945–1991)

- **1945–1949** — Chinese Civil War concludes with Communist victory.
- **1947–1949** — First Indo-Pakistani War (Kashmir).
- **1948–1949** — Arab–Israeli War (Israeli War of Independence).
- **1950–1953** — Korean War.
- **1946–1954** — First Indochina War (France vs. Viet Minh).
- **1954–1962** — Algerian War of Independence.
- **1956** — Suez Crisis; Hungarian Revolution (Soviet intervention).
- **1955–1975** — Vietnam War (Second Indochina War).
- **1962** — Cuban Missile Crisis (peak nuclear confrontation); Sino-Indian War.
- **1965** — Second Indo-Pakistani War.
- **1967** — Six-Day War (Arab–Israeli).
- **1971** — Bangladesh Liberation War / Third Indo-Pakistani War.
- **1973** — Yom Kippur War.
- **1979–1989** — Soviet–Afghan War.
- **1980–1988** — Iran–Iraq War.
- **1982** — Falklands War (UK vs. Argentina).

---

## Post-Cold War to Present (1991–2026)

- **1990–1991** — Gulf War (Operation Desert Storm).
- **1991–2001** — Yugoslav Wars (including Bosnia, Kosovo).
- **1994** — Rwandan Genocide (with major military dimensions).
- **1999** — Kargil War (India–Pakistan); NATO intervention in Kosovo.
- **2001–2021** — War in Afghanistan (U.S.-led coalition vs. Taliban / al-Qaeda).
- **2003–2011** — Iraq War (invasion and subsequent insurgency).
- **2006** — Second Lebanon War.
- **2008** — Russo-Georgian War.
- **2011** — Libyan Civil War / NATO intervention; Syrian Civil War begins.
- **2014–present** — Russo-Ukrainian conflict escalates (Crimea, Donbas); full-scale Russian invasion of Ukraine begins February 2022.
- **2015–present** — Yemeni Civil War (with Saudi-led coalition involvement).
- **2020** — Second Nagorno-Karabakh War.
- **2023–present** — Israel–Hamas war (Gaza) and related regional escalations; continued fighting in Ukraine, Sudan, and other theaters.

---

## Notes

- Casualty figures and exact start/end dates are often disputed; this timeline prioritizes widely recognized major phases.
- Many conflicts overlap or have multiple phases (civil wars, insurgencies, proxy wars).
- Nuclear weapons have not been used in combat since 1945, but nuclear deterrence has shaped strategy ever since.
- This page will continue to grow. Suggestions for additions are welcome via pull requests.

*Compiled from open historical sources for educational reference.*
